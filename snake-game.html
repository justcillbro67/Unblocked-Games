How to Play

Guide the snake to eat glowing orbs. Each orb adds length and increases speed. Colliding with walls or yourself ends the run.

Controls

Arrow Keys WASD P = Pause Swipe (Mobile)

Tips

Take wide turns at higher speeds. Use the grid edges to reset your route and avoid tight loops.
